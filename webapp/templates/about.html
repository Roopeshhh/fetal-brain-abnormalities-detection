Clinical Engineering · System Architecture

An Explainable Computer-Aided Diagnostic (CAD) platform combining hierarchical Vision Transformers with Digital Image Processing for automated fetal neuro-sonographic screening.

Project Objective & Clinical Context

Congenital central nervous system (CNS) anomalies occur in approximately 1 to 2 per 1,000 live births and represent a major cause of pediatric morbidity. Traditional ultrasound screening relies heavily on sonographer expertise and image interpretation under challenging acoustic conditions. This system implements a fine-tuned Swin Transformer backbone to perform automated 15-class differential diagnosis, generating real-time Gradient-weighted Class Activation Maps (Grad-CAM) to validate anatomical focal regions and reduce diagnostic variability.

Core Diagnostic Modules

Integrated pipelines engineered for clinical ultrasound analysis

Swin Transformer Backbone

Hierarchical shifted-window self-attention with linear computational complexity $O(M \times N)$ relative to image size, extracting multi-scale axial fetal brain features.

Explainable Grad-CAM

Hooks into Stage-4 LayerNorm to backpropagate class-specific activation gradients, synthesizing a normalized Jet colormap highlighting suspicious anatomical ROIs.

Signal Quality & DIP Metrics

Performs chromatic divergence checks to reject non-ultrasound files and extracts acoustic features: Mean Echo Intensity, Contrast Variance, and Shannon Texture Entropy.

End-to-End Processing Pipeline

From raw B-mode ultrasound ingestion to formal medical report

Dual-Scan Visual Workspace

Renders a synchronized vertical inspection stack displaying the original B-mode axial ultrasound scan aligned directly above the Stage-4 Grad-CAM activation heatmap for rapid cross-referencing.

Clinical Diagnostic PDF Reports

ReportLab-driven automated PDF generation embedding patient demographics, primary diagnosis, ranked Top-5 probabilities, DIP metrics, and side-by-side scan visualizations.
